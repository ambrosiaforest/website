welcome

welcome to my cozy little site. this is my personal website where i will post about things that interest me. my goal is to keep this website simple and 100% javascript free. this site aims to mimic the good era of sites with minimal bloat and people would just post stuff they were interested in.
this site is run on my homeserver running openbsd with their httpd web server and relayd reverse proxy. i like openbsd because of how minimal it is while also having decent security.
hope to see you all around :3
